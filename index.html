02Fuel & safety

A practical note: fuel costs and safety ratings.

On a practical note, you should know what the Kona costs to feed, and how it does on safety.

Fuel. The EPA rates the Kona at 20 mpg city and 27 mpg highway, for a combined 23 mpg. Confirm the exact rating for any model year at fueleconomy.gov.

  • Thirstier than a base Kona, and it should be. You're paying in fuel for the turbo and the drive that comes with it.
  • It's tuned for premium. Feed it 87-octane regular to save a few dollars a tank and you're working against the engine it was built to be.
  • Check the fuel grade on the window sticker of the specific build.

Safety. The standard Kona platform has performed well in IIHS testing, and NHTSA publishes crash-test ratings and open recall campaigns by VIN.

  • We check every unit before it lists. We pulled the VIN history and checked the NHTSA recall database on each Kona we took in this summer, before it ever went on the line.
  • Open campaigns get cleared first. If a unit had one, we cleared it with Hyundai before listing.
  • A clean Carfax and a clean recall record beats rushing a car onto the lot.

05Ownership

What ownership looks like here.

Buying the car is one part of the picture; the years after are the other. Any car deserves a service department that knows the brand, and ours is on-site at the same National City location.

Our service department is open Monday through Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Two things we offer make day-to-day Kona ownership easier:

  • Dalton VIP Advantage Program. Our ownership program built around customers who buy and service with us.
  • Complimentary In-Drive Alignment Check and Tire Check every visit. Every time your Kona comes in, we check alignment and tires at no charge, and that's exactly the kind of upkeep that protects your tires and keeps it handling right.

Why does the free alignment check matter so much on this car specifically? Alignment drifts on any car that meets curbs, potholes and hard cornering.

  • It goes out of spec easily. A clipped curb on a San Diego parking-structure ramp, a pothole on a National City side street, or a few hard launches can nudge your alignment out.
  • You will feel it. On a car this sensitive it shows up as a tug in the wheel, then as ragged inner-edge tire wear weeks later.
  • Performance tires are not cheap to replace early. Catching a half-degree of toe before it eats $800 worth of rubber is precisely what a complimentary check pays for itself on.
  • Parts on hand. Our parts department stocks or orders genuine Hyundai components for it, Monday through Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Is the Kona or the regular Kona the better buy?

It depends on how you drive. If you want quickness and sharp handling and don't mind a firm ride and premium fuel, the Kona is worth the premium.

If you mostly commute and want better mpg and a softer ride, the standard Kona or Kona Hybrid is the smarter buy, and we'll happily point you there.
