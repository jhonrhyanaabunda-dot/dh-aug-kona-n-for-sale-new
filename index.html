01From the sales floor

The Kona N at a glance.

The Kona N isn't for drivers who want a basic commuter. It's for those who want a hot SUV.

It's Hyundai's N-division take on the Kona: a small SUV built to be quick and sharp through corners while still hauling people and gear like a crossover should. Here's what the N badge gets you:

  • 276 hp from a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder, with a brief overboost that lifts torque to 289 lb-ft.
  • 0-to-60 in roughly 5.0 seconds with the eight-speed wet dual-clutch transmission and launch control.
  • An electronically controlled limited-slip differential, adaptive dampers, and bigger brakes than any other Kona.

02Fuel & safety

A practical note: fuel costs and safety ratings.

Knowing the performance numbers is great, but on a more practical and responsible note, you should know: what does it cost to feed, and is it safe?

Fuel. The EPA rates the Kona N at 20 mpg city and 27 mpg highway, for a combined 23 mpg. Confirm the exact rating for any model year at fueleconomy.gov.

  • Thirstier than a base Kona, and it should be. You're paying in fuel for the turbo and the drive that comes with it.
  • It's tuned for premium. Feed it 87-octane regular to save a few dollars a tank and you're working against the engine it was built to be.
  • Budget for premium if you're set on the N.

Safety. The standard Kona platform has performed well in IIHS testing, and NHTSA publishes crash-test ratings and open recall campaigns by VIN.

  • We check every unit before it lists. We pulled the VIN history and checked the NHTSA recall database on each Kona N we took in this summer, before it ever went on the line.
  • Open campaigns get cleared first. If a unit had one, we cleared it with Hyundai before listing.
  • A clean Carfax and a clean recall record beats rushing a car onto the lot.

03On the lot

Our walk-around inspection.

When a Kona N lands at National City, our team makes sure everything works as it was engineered to do.

On our most recent arrival, we checked it corner to corner.

  • Front tires. Inspected for the inner-edge wear that aggressive cornering and a tight turning radius can cause.
  • Eight-speed DCT. Run through its drive modes on a short loop to confirm launch control and rev-match engaged cleanly.
  • N Grin Shift. We verified the overboost button did what Hyundai says it does.
  • What we found. On one trade-in Kona N, uneven front tire wear, so we replaced the front pair before listing it. Nobody should inherit somebody else's track-day habits at full price.

That hands-on pass is also why our complimentary alignment check matters more on this car than on the standard commuter Kona. More on that below.

What ownership looks like here.

Buying the car is one part of the picture; the years after are the other. A performance model deserves a service department that knows the brand, and ours is on-site at the same National City location.

Our service department is open Monday through Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Two things we offer make day-to-day Kona N ownership easier:

  • Dalton VIP Advantage Program. Our ownership program built around customers who buy and service with us.
  • Complimentary In-Drive Alignment Check and Tire Check every visit. Every time your Kona N comes in, we check alignment and tires at no charge, and on a vehicle driven the way an N is meant to be driven, that's exactly the kind of upkeep that protects your tires and keeps it handling right.

Why does the free alignment check matter so much on this car specifically? The Kona N rides on summer-biased performance rubber and a sport-tuned suspension.

  • It goes out of spec easily. A clipped curb on a San Diego parking-structure ramp, a pothole on a National City side street, or a few hard launches can nudge your alignment out.
  • You will feel it. On a car this sensitive it shows up as a tug in the wheel, then as ragged inner-edge tire wear weeks later.
  • Performance tires are not cheap to replace early. Catching a half-degree of toe before it eats $800 worth of rubber is precisely what a complimentary check pays for itself on.
  • Parts on hand. Our parts department stocks or orders genuine Hyundai components for it, Monday through Friday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

How much horsepower does the Kona N have?

Is the Kona N or the regular Kona the better buy?

It depends on how you drive. If you want quickness and sharp handling and don't mind a firm ride and premium fuel, the Kona N is worth the premium.

If you mostly commute and want better mpg and a softer ride, the standard Kona or Kona Hybrid is the smarter buy, and we'll happily point you there.
